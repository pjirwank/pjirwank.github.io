Piyush P. Jirwankar

I study hybrid dynamical systems on manifolds and develop tools for their analysis, safe control, and estimation.

I work with Professor Ricardo Sanfelice at the Hybrid Systems Laboratory, University of California, Santa Cruz. My research connects geometric control theory with robotics and autonomous systems.
