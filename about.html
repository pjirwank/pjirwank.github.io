Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of California, Santa Cruz, working with Prof. Ricardo Sanfelice in the Hybrid Systems Laboratory.

My research focuses on hybrid dynamical systems, which combine continuous evolution with discrete transitions. I study systems on manifolds and develop theoretical tools for stability analysis, safe control, obstacle avoidance, and attitude estimation.

Before joining UC Santa Cruz, I earned a dual degree at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay in 2022, with a B.Tech. in Aerospace Engineering and an M.Tech. in Systems and Control Engineering.
